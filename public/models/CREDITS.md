# uma.glb credits

`uma.glb` is built from the MakeHuman 1.1 system assets: the base mesh and its targets, the default
skeleton, the young light-skinned male skin texture (re-tinted), the short01 hair, eyebrow001, Eyelashes01,
HighPolyEyes (brown) and male_elegantsuit01 (recoloured and cut into a dobok).

MakeHuman releases the assets bundled with MakeHuman 1.1 under CC0
(http://www.makehumancommunity.org; the licence statement is in MakeHuman's own source, `makehuman.py`).
Models exported from MakeHuman are free to use. The JSON copies were taken from the `makehuman-data-v1`
npm package. Thanks to the MakeHuman team: Manuel Bastioni, Jonas Hauquier, Thomas Larsson and everyone
listed at http://www.makehumancommunity.org.

The belt, the beard and scalp painting and the dobok recolouring were made for this site. Build scripts are
in `scripts/avatar/`.
